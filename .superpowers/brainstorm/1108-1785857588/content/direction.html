Worth knowing before you pick

The whole reason this repo exists is SEO and Core Web Vitals — the Laravel site was losing to CLS and blocking CSS. Option C spends that budget back. Digital Silk's page is heavy; ours is currently fully static with almost no client JS. B gets ~90% of the visual impact at ~20% of the performance cost, which is why I'd take it. If you want cinematic touches, the safer route is B now, then add one or two pinned sequences afterwards and measure.
